About TypeHaven

A minimal, distraction-free typing test inspired by Monkeytype’s clean design.

  • Modes: time, words, quotes, code, numbers, zen
  • Live WPM & accuracy
  • 10+ themes + full custom theme editor
  • Sound effects & multiple caret styles
  • Personal bests in localStorage
  • One-click share to X / Discord / clipboard
  • Keyboard-first (Tab + Enter to restart)

Deploy to Netlify or Vercel as a static site. Real social auto-post (OAuth) can be added later.
